# Find the Imposter

A party game of strategy and bluffing. Everyone gets the secret word — except the imposter. Discuss, deduce, and vote to expose the imposter before they bluff their way to victory.

- 🎭 Easy to learn, quick to play
- 📚 Multiple categories and difficulty levels
- 📴 Works fully offline — perfect for game nights, road trips, and parties

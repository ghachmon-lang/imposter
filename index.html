Imposter

Everyone knows the secret word.
Except one of you.

Pass & play · works offline · 3–12 players

How to Play

  1. Deal the roles. Pass the phone around. Everyone secretly sees the secret word — except the imposter, who sees nothing.
  2. Give clues. Going around the circle, each player says one word related to the secret word. The imposter has to improvise and blend in.
  3. Discuss. Too vague? Too obvious? Debate who doesn't seem to know the word.
  4. Vote. Point at your suspect on three, then record the group's accusation.
  5. Reveal. If the imposter is caught, they get one chance to guess the secret word and steal the win. If an innocent player is accused, the imposter wins.

💡 Tip: clues that are too specific hand the word to the imposter — clues that are too vague make you look like the imposter.
